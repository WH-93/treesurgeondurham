If you have had a tree removed, the stump is usually the last thing left. Stump grinding grinds it below ground level so the area can be re-turfed, seeded or paved. Here is what it costs and what to expect.

## Typical stump grinding costs in Durham

| Stump size | Typical range | Notes |
|-----------|--------------|-------|
| Small (up to 30cm across), e.g. apple or birch | £50 - £100 | Done in under an hour |
| Medium (30-60cm), e.g. most garden trees | £100 - £180 | Most common job |
| Large (60-90cm), e.g. mature sycamore or ash | £180 - £300 | May need the bigger machine |
| Extra large (90cm+) or multiple stumps | From £300 | Quoted per site |

Grinding several stumps in one visit works out cheaper per stump, because the machine is already on site.

## What affects the price

- **Diameter at the cut.** This sets the machine size and the time.
- **Roots and soil.** Big surface roots add work. Rocky ground is harder on the machine.
- **Access.** A stump at the front of a garden next to a drive is quick. One at the bottom of a steep bank, carried to by hand, is slower.
- **What happens to the grindings.** We can spread them over the area or take them away. If we take them, it costs a little more.

## After the grind

The stump is removed to below ground level, so the top of the hole can be filled with soil and re-turfed or seeded. A small depression is normal while the soil settles. There is no need to dig out the roots, and the surrounding lawn usually recovers within a season.

## Stump grinding or stump removal?

- **Grinding** grinds the stump below surface level and leaves the ground usable. Cheaper, faster, and what most people want.
- **Removal** digs the whole stump and root ball out. Needed only if you are building on the exact spot, paving over it, or planting something new in the same place.
